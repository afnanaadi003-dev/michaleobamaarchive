Biography & Early Foundations

1964 – Present

South Side Chicago Roots

Michelle LaVaughn Robinson was born on January 17, 1964, in Chicago, Illinois, to Fraser Robinson III, a city water plant pump operator, and Marian Shield Robinson, a secretary. Raised in a two-bedroom apartment on Euclid Avenue in South Shore, her parents instilled an uncompromising work ethic and emphasis on higher education.

Her father continued working daily despite battling multiple sclerosis, serving as a lifelong model of perseverance and quiet dignity for Michelle and her older brother, Craig.

Princeton & Harvard Academic Excellence

Michelle attended Whitney M. Young Magnet High School before earning her B.A. cum laude in Sociology from Princeton University in 1985 (with a minor in African American Studies). She earned her Juris Doctor (J.D.) from Harvard Law School in 1988.

Her Princeton senior thesis examined ethnic identity and social integration, laying early groundwork for her lifelong advocacy for educational equity.

Personal Chapter

Family Values, Partnership & Love Story

The Sidley Austin Meeting (1989)

Michelle Robinson was assigned to advisor/mentor summer associate Barack Obama at the Sidley Austin law firm in Chicago in June 1989. Initially hesitant to date a colleague, she agreed to a summer date visiting the Art Institute of Chicago, taking a long walk, and watching Spike Lee's Do the Right Thing.

They married on October 3, 1992, at Trinity United Church of Christ in Chicago.

Raising Malia & Sasha in the Spotlight

During eight years in the White House, Michelle prioritized ensuring daughters Malia (born 1998) and Sasha (born 2001) maintained grounded, normal childhoods—insisting on bedtimes, household chores, and limited media exposure despite Secret Service presence.

Her mother, Marian Robinson, lived with the family in the Executive Residence, serving as a steadfast anchor for the young first daughters.
